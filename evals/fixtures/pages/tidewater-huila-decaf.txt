COLOMBIA HUILA DECAF (Sugarcane EA)
250 g

Notes of milk chocolate, red apple and panela. A decaf you'd happily drink at 9 am.

Decaffeinated in Colombia using ethyl acetate made from fermented sugarcane, a gentle process that keeps the coffee's sweetness. The green comes from smallholders around Pitalito.

Origin: Colombia
Region: Huila (Pitalito)
Varieties: Caturra, Castillo
Altitude: 1,600 – 1,800 m
Process: Sugarcane ethyl acetate (EA) decaffeination, washed
Roast: Medium
Brew: espresso and filter
