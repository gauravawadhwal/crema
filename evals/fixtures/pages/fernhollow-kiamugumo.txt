KIRINYAGA KIAMUGUMO AA
$24.00 – 250 g

Blackcurrant · Pink grapefruit · Demerara

Kiamugumo is a washing station on the slopes of Mount Kenya, serving around 1,200 smallholder members of the Baragwi Farmers' Cooperative. Cherry is hand-sorted on arrival, pulped, fermented overnight, washed in clean river water and dried on raised beds for up to three weeks.

Country: Kenya
Region: Kirinyaga
Producer: Baragwi Farmers' Cooperative Society
Varieties: SL28, SL34, Ruiru 11
Process: Washed, double fermented
Altitude: 1,700 – 1,850 masl
Roast: Light
Roasted for: Filter and espresso (our omni roast)

ESPRESSO RECIPE
We dial this in at 19 g in, 45 g out, in 30–34 seconds at 94°C. Expect a juicy, high-acid shot that sweetens as it cools.

Shipping: we roast Monday and Thursday and ship the same day.
