How it works

  • 1Read the bag. Roaster, name and roast date. Only the bag has the date for your lot.
  • 2Find the roaster's page. Origin, variety, process, altitude, tasting notes, their photo and any recipe they publish.
  • 3Match a profile. The roaster's description is matched against community profiles, then the AI picks one and explains why.
